01 · Executive Summary

What Confair is — and why it wins

Confair is a workforce partner for aviation, maritime and offshore energy that combines recruitment, contracting, compliance, onboarding, workforce management and payroll into one connected, technology-enabled process — not six disconnected services.

Founded in aviation in 2007, Confair has spent 15+ years operating in one of the world's most regulated environments. That discipline — certification, compliance, deployment under pressure — now extends across maritime and offshore energy, where the same challenge exists: internationally mobile, certified professionals who must be sourced, verified, deployed and paid without error or delay.

Clients don't buy recruitment. They buy operational continuity — aircraft that fly, vessels that sail, turbines that turn, payroll that's right, compliance that holds up to audit. Everything Confair builds serves that outcome.

02 · Our Story

Built on expertise, driven by people

Confair was born in aviation — the discipline of that industry shaped everything. It was never meant to stay there.

Confair was founded in 2007 in IJmuiden, focused on aviation workforce solutions. New ownership in 2015 began the expansion into multiple industries; the headquarters moved to Utrecht in 2016. By 2018 Confair had deployed 2,000+ professionals globally. In 2021 the company expanded into offshore renewable energy (offshore wind), and in 2022 incorporated Confair Middle East (UAE) to support regional operations. By 2024 Confair was operating in 40+ countries with 3,000+ professionals deployed.

That history matters commercially: aviation is one of the most demanding, tightly regulated workforce environments on earth. Proving the model there — under EASA, FAA and ICAO scrutiny — is the credibility that lets Confair say it can handle maritime and offshore energy with the same rigour.

07 · Continuum — The Platform & Operational Intelligence

What we've actually built

We call it Continuum — Confair's own connected workforce platform. This is not a slide-deck vision: these capabilities run in production today, which is what lets Confair claim the "platform, not agency" position credibly.

Recruitment & ATS

Confair's own applicant & candidate system feeds vacancies straight to the website and pulls applications back — one pipeline, not a job-board reposting exercise.

Compliance & onboarding

A verification-gated onboarding tool (proven in aviation, e.g. cabin-crew programmes) that orchestrates document collection, training, IT, travel and reporting across every stakeholder.

Roster → payroll engine

Operational data becomes payment data automatically: rosters, flight hours, duty types, allowances, overtime and CLA rules convert to multi-currency payroll — the capability most rivals lack entirely.

Invoicing & finance

Placement and company invoicing, expenses, recurring billing, SEPA payment files and accounting export — the money side of the workforce, connected to the same data.

Client & candidate portals

A dashboard PWA for the operations team plus candidate-facing flows — the foundation for the real-time visibility clients want and the self-service professionals expect.

Operational intelligence The differentiator

Because every stage writes to one record in Continuum, Confair can surface availability, mobilisation lead times, rate benchmarks, compliance risk and redeployment candidates — decisions, not just data.

Why this is the moat. Recruitment firms own relationships. Confair owns the operating data of a workforce. Once a client's rosters, compliance records and payroll run through Continuum, switching means rebuilding their operational backbone — which is exactly why infrastructure companies become embedded and recruitment companies compete on price.

How our model compares to the market

Every rival offers a slice of this. The pattern: they specialise in one delivery model and run it on manual back-offices. Confair's move is to match each model — then connect them.

Contract & permanent staffing
Airswift · NES · GOOSE · Zenon · Brookfield

Table stakes — everyone does it. Confair: offered, but framed as the way in, not the product. We compete above it on what happens after placement.

Crew leasing / EOR
Rishworth · CAE Parc · NES

Sticky, high-value — Confair employs the professional and carries the admin. Confair: yes, and automated end-to-end in Continuum, versus their admin-heavy manual back-offices — a direct wedge.

Managed solutions (MSP / RPO)
NES "Advantage" · GOOSE campaigns

Enterprise clients want a partner to run the programme. Confair: the clearest offering to formalise — the platform gives real-time visibility incumbents can't, making a managed service defensible.

Train-to-deploy / certification
CAE Parc (sim) · Resource Group (Part-147) · Anglo-Eastern (academy)

Owning the training pipeline is a talent moat. Confair: partner rather than build — secure vetted supply through training alliances without the capex.

Adjacent services (travel, insurance, payments)
V.Group · BSM (NiumPay crew fintech)

Incumbents bundle broadly (ship management, catering, insurance). Confair: stay focused on workforce — selectively add travel/mobilisation (already in scope) and embedded crew payments as a retention lever.

The integrated advantage. A client can buy any one service from a dozen firms. What none of them can offer is all of it running on one record — recruit, verify, deploy, operate and pay without the data ever leaving the system. That's the difference between a supplier and infrastructure.

What the market teaches us

The whitespace
Nobody occupies this

Cross-sector mobility. Every scaled rival is single-vertical (V.Group, BSM, GOOSE, Rishworth, Zenon, CAE Parc, Brookfield) or a broad generalist that dilutes the safety-critical niche (Airswift, NES). Aviation + maritime + offshore together, with one profile that redeploys across them, is Confair's to claim — and Atlas (marine+energy only) is the closest anyone comes.

Adopt: a flagship report
Airswift GETI · GOOSE Pilot Survey

The proven lead-gen and SEO engine in this industry is an annual, branded data report. Airswift's GETI (9,000+ professionals, 143 countries) and GOOSE's Pilot Survey dominate search and PR. No offshore or maritime player has a strong equivalent — an open goal for a cross-sector Confair index.

Adopt: real self-service
V.Crew Connect · BSM Portal · Airswift Digital

Candidate/crew apps (documents, expiry, availability, e-sign, payslips) are now baseline in maritime and at Airswift — but entirely absent across aviation crewing. Confair's portal + records tooling is a genuine differentiator there, and table stakes in maritime. BSM's embedded crew fintech (multi-currency wallet, instant pay) is worth studying as a retention lever.

Avoid
The cautionary tales

Multi-brand sprawl (V.Group) fragments UX — keep one coherent identity. Over-broadening (Airswift into tech/infra) dilutes the niche — stay sharp on safety-critical. Pure job-board plays commoditise; compete on tooling and data, not listing volume.

Activation checklist — turn the wiring on

  1. Confirm HubSpot is live in production — service key set, deal pipeline & stage IDs configured on the API. Until the token is set the lead webhook logs "disabled" and nothing syncs (though Supabase still captures leads for backfill).
  2. Add the custom UTM properties in HubSpot (source/medium/campaign/term/content) so pipeline can be attributed to campaigns, not just read on the contact note.
  3. Ship the flagship report as the anchor lead magnet; gate it through the existing guide/lead flow.
  4. Stand up 6–10 pillar/compliance pages targeting real search demand per vertical, interlinked to vacancies and the report.
  5. Wire outbound — Apollo/Instantly for cold-email sequencing and LinkedIn Sales Navigator, all tagged with the UTM scheme so outbound-sourced pipeline is measurable.
  6. Instrument the loop — a simple HubSpot dashboard: leads by source, MQL→SQL rate, pipeline by campaign. Optimise what you can now see.
